## Goals

<!-- tars-question-id: goals -->
### What outcomes should this project achieve?

I want to plan a holiday to New Zealand for me and my family of 4.

## Values

<!-- tars-question-id: values -->
### What values should guide how the team works and makes decisions?

We value a holiday that feels memorable, enjoyable for the whole family, and low-stress to plan and experience.

We value balance: enough adventure to make the trip special, with enough comfort and downtime for everyone to enjoy it.

We value making choices that respect our budget without losing the experience we want.

## Principles

<!-- tars-question-id: principles -->
### What practical principles should keep the team aligned when choices are difficult?

Plan around the needs of all four family members, not just the cheapest or most exciting option.

Prioritize a manageable itinerary with realistic travel times and built-in rest.

Choose experiences that offer strong value for money over trying to do everything.

Leave room for flexibility so the trip can adapt if the weather, energy, or plans change.

When we have to trade off, favor shared family enjoyment over squeezing in extra activities.
